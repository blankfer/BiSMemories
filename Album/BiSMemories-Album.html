BiS> Memories — your album

Point this at your Screenshots folder and it becomes an album you can click through. Everything happens inside your browser: nothing is uploaded, nothing is installed, and it works offline. Add the addon's saved file too and each picture gets its story.

Nothing loaded yet. Click Choose your Screenshots folder above, or drag the folder onto this page.

1. The pictures — your Screenshots folder:

C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\Screenshots

Replace _classic_beta_ with whichever you play: _classic_era_, _classic_, _retail_. If you installed WoW somewhere else, it is the Screenshots folder beside that WTF one.

2. The captions — the addon's saved file. This one is NOT in your AddOns folder. It lives in WTF, which is where WoW keeps everything it remembers about you:

C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_\WTF\Account\<ACCOUNT>\SavedVariables\BiSMemories.lua

<ACCOUNT> is whatever WoW called your account folder — a number and a hash like 213614#1, or a name like ARNOLD891. It is never your email.
There may be more than one, and the extra ones are often nearly empty. People with several accounts have several folders, and anyone who plays two at once certainly does. Open each until you find the one that actually contains SavedVariables\BiSMemories.lua — that is yours. If more than one has it, take the one changed most recently.
There is a second copy under your realm and character (…\Account\<ACCOUNT>\<Realm>\<Character>\SavedVariables\) and either works — the one above is simplest because it holds every character.

Your browser may ask permission to read the folder. That is the browser protecting you, not this page asking for anything — say yes and the pictures are read from disk and shown. They never leave your machine.
