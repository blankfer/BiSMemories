Drag your WoW folder onto this page

1. Copy where your game is:

2. Press the Windows key + E to open File Explorer (the yellow folder window — not a browser tab). Click its address bar at the top, paste, and press Enter.

3. Drag the folder you see there onto this page.

Open C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft in File Explorer and drag the _classic_beta_ folder here. That is all: the page finds your pictures and the addon's notes inside it by itself.

WoW is somewhere else, or it does not work?

It is the folder with Screenshots and WTF in it — on another drive it might be D:\Games\World of Warcraft\_classic_beta_. You can also drop the Screenshots folder and the notes file separately; the notes are WTF\Account\(your account)\SavedVariables\BiSMemories.lua.

Cannot drag? Pick the folder instead:

Chrome will then ask to "upload" thousands of files. That is its word for handing them to this page — nothing leaves your computer, and this page works with the internet unplugged. Dragging never asks.

Captions appear after you log out or /reload in game — that is when WoW writes the notes down.
