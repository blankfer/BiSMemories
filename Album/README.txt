BiS Memories - your album
=========================

The addon takes the pictures and writes down what each one was. This page turns them into an
album you can click through.


HOW TO USE IT
-------------

1. Double-click  BiSMemories-Album.html  in this folder. It opens in your browser.

2. In File Explorer, open  C:\Program Files (x86)\World of Warcraft  and drag the
   _classic_beta_  folder onto the page. (Or click "or pick the folder" and choose it.)

That is all. The page finds your pictures (in Screenshots) and the addon's notes (in WTF) by
itself, and says what it found. If there is more than one account folder in WTF, it takes the
notes that were saved most recently.

Click a picture to see it full size, the arrow keys move between them, Escape or the X closes it.


NEXT TIME, NO DRAGGING
----------------------

After one drag the page knows where your pictures and notes are. From then on, just open the
page and your album is there - with the notes as of your last logout or /reload. A picture you
took yourself (Print Screen) since your last drag shows after the next drag.

"Make a desktop icon" (at the top, once the album is open) puts a shortcut on your desktop that
opens this page. Double-click it and you are in your album.

The page shows you where your game folder is, with a Copy button: paste it into File Explorer's
address bar to get straight to the folder you drag.


FINDING ONE PICTURE
-------------------

Search the box at the top for a zone, a boss, an item, a level, a player who was with you.
Beside it is a row of counts: "12 boss", "12 death", "18 new place". Click one to see only those.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------

* Nothing is uploaded and nothing is installed. The page reads the files off your disk and works
  with the internet unplugged. When Chrome says it will "upload" files, it means hand them to
  this page - they never leave your computer.

* Captions appear after you log out or /reload in game: that is when WoW writes the notes down.

* WoW somewhere else? It is the folder with Screenshots and WTF in it. You can also drop the
  Screenshots folder and the notes file (WTF\Account\<your account>\SavedVariables\BiSMemories.lua)
  separately.

* Chrome, Edge and Firefox all work. Safari is patchy with folders; use another browser.
